Every country and territory

Meet. Share. Learn. Teach. Leave enough room for the next worthwhile invitation.

An open journey

An ambition that can include returns, longer stays and changes of direction.

Sire wants to visit every country and territory, with time to meet people, share what he knows, learn something and teach where he can be useful.

A place deserves more than a tick. A return visit may matter more than the next border. An invitation can make a longer stay worthwhile, and new work can give a journey a different purpose.

An oracle that brings things together

The travel oracle considers invitations, creative opportunities, events, seasons, transport, resources and existing commitments. It also makes room for energy, changing interests and the simple desire to spend more time somewhere.

It can explain why a possibility deserves attention. A reading, a workshop and a music gathering might fit into one visit. Another invitation might be better saved for a return. Sire chooses how to respond.

Right place, right time

In the fiction, serendipity can be extraordinary. Practical travel still involves the details that make participation possible: when something happens, what a visit requires and what he can realistically offer.
