# Australian Sire: current visual brief

This site gives a personal hope and wish a fictional life. Luke’s stated preferences and the narratives in his Story Forge guide the casting and scene choices.

Sire is not the settling-down kind. Continuing travel is central. Global Group Marriages is a main series exploration, with relationships across people and places. Shared-home imagery depicts a visit or a part of that wider life; do not imply his story ends in permanent settlement. Show arrivals, departures, shared journeys and reunions as well as time spent together.

## Characters and atmosphere

- Female romantic characters are adults aged 22 to 45.
- Very full busts are a deliberate, visible character-design preference. Preserve this specificity in the casting and properly fitted clothing.
- Visibly pregnant women belong in the imagined social world and shared-life scenes.
- Women begin the personal connection. Show an invitation, question, gesture or shared joke, with Sire responding.
- Sire retains Luke’s recognisable mature face, rectangular glasses, current long wavy brown-grey hair and natural build.
- The requested register is classy, fun, social and pre-intimate. Show anticipation through expressions, company, conversation and setting, with fully clothed adults.
- Give each woman a distinct appearance, role and activity. Show a whole social situation with tangible objects, useful spaces and something happening.
- Bring a future-forward character to the spaces and clothes: useful Aura devices, responsive architecture, advanced textiles, creative instruments and inhabited green cities. Keep the future welcoming, practical and socially alive.

## Source connections

The Siren and music lead to an after-performance greenroom invitation. Sire’s writer role and books as a signal lead to readers approaching him. Luxurious shared spaces, food, care and future relationships lead to a coastal-home dinner. These are newly staged scenes using the narrative ingredients, not claims about real events.

## Across the site

Every content image has one placement. Use different settings, actions, clothes, viewpoints and colour combinations. Preserve the readability of faces and gestures in phone crops. Keep long text off the artwork. Captions identify generated story concepts.

The original photos are likeness references and remain outside the repository. This file records the current project brief; historical generation prompts retain their original wording.
