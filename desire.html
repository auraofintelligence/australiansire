What draws him

Particular desires. Mutual curiosity. The possibility of sharing a much larger life.

Desire and personality

His personal tastes and the relationships he hopes to explore.

Sire has particular tastes and expansive hopes for where desire might lead. His fiction explores attraction alongside travel, group marriage and the possibility of sharing life across countries.

The attraction is specific

Sire has a pronounced attraction to breasts, particularly very large breasts: mazophilia and a preference for macromastia. His breeding fetish brings fertility, pregnancy and the thought of conceiving a child with a woman who shares that desire into his romantic imagination.

He also enjoys a quick wit, warmth, confidence, intelligence and the pleasure of someone being direct about what she wants. An unexpected question draws him into a conversation. A shared interest keeps them talking. Her invitation takes him somewhere he had never thought to imagine.

Where desire leads him

He imagines several women he loves and desires, pregnancies, fatherhood and reunions across countries. The anticipation of seeing a lover again belongs alongside the excitement of a first invitation. Global Group Marriages explores the larger relationships within that appetite.

The rest of the evening matters

Music, food, clothes, movement and place all change the mood. So does the difference between watching a performer across a crowded room and talking with her after the audience has gone home.

There is room for opulence and for ordinary comfort: an extraordinary dress, a good meal, a laugh over a badly behaved piece of technology, a quiet place to talk. He enjoys the physical world as well as the ideas running through it.

A personal exploration

He enjoys the uncertainty of meeting someone new. An unexpected question catches him off guard. A laugh changes the mood. He finds himself wondering what else she has in mind.

Consent and enjoyment are part of the relationships he wants. The women he meets have their own reasons for approaching him, and their invitations help shape what happens next.
