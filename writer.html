The writer behind Sire

Luke Nathan Hayes is building a story world around desire, intelligence, travel and the possibilities of a good life.

Luke Nathan Hayes

The person, the character and the work in progress.

Australian Sire is close to Luke’s interests and intentions. Fiction gives those interests room to travel into extraordinary places and lives.

Luke’s work ranges across practical experience, AI philosophy, self-reflection, conscious cognitive architecture and creative worldbuilding. The question of what a joyful, responsible and abundant life could look like runs through these projects.

Putting himself into the work

The writing is personal. Luke wants his science-fiction romantasy to express enough of his personality and desires that women who naturally share an affinity can discover him through it.

Sire is a writer too. That connection lets the fiction explore what happens when a story becomes the beginning of a conversation, an invitation or a relationship.

Follow the threads

The Man and Mind site gives more room to Luke’s thinking. His world of work experience adds practical history. The Story Forge opens the developing fictional universe, with a broad range of possible scenes and connected stories.
