Meet the character

An adult science-fiction romantasy universe by Luke Nathan Hayes.

Australian Sire is not the settling-down kind of guy. He wants to travel to every country and territory, write, meet, share, learn and teach. His relationships belong within that moving life.

He enjoys being alive. Good conversation, a woman’s interest, a ridiculous idea worth trying, a place he has never been. He has room in his life for all of it.

He is Australian, a writer and a traveller. Long hair, glasses, a ready curiosity and a sense of humour sit comfortably alongside very particular desires. He can enjoy a grand setting without needing to be the grandest person in it.

A life that leaves room

He writes, makes things, learns and contributes. His stories explore the lives, relationships and futures that interest him. They also let women discover what he is about, in their own time.

Women initiate his first personal encounters. A woman or women open the conversation, make an offer or invite him somewhere unexpected. He responds. An ordinary afternoon can become the beginning of a very different story.

The invitation arrives while he is getting on with living.

He is drawn to colour, pleasure, capability and people who enjoy exploring an idea. He has bold ambitions and an appetite for intimacy, with the humility to listen when someone opens a door into a life he knows nothing about.

Global Group Marriages

Global Group Marriages is one of the series’ main explorations. Desire, love, marriage, pregnancy, children and shared commitments can connect lives across countries. Sire keeps travelling within that possibility, with room for new invitations, familiar faces and relationships that continue between visits.

Who travels together? Who welcomes him back? What can they share across distance? Those questions run through the books, alongside the attraction and pleasure of meeting in the first place.

A much larger horizon

His world can hold a hidden mountain city, an ocean civilisation, a shared home and a kitchen table covered in manuscript pages. The extraordinary grows out of the personal: someone speaks to him, he listens, and another possibility becomes visible.

Joyful responsible abundance is the life he wants to explore. Time to create. The means to travel. Good food, music, affection, useful work and relationships with room to grow.

The conversation starts on shore.

He has come in from the waves. Two women stop to talk, and one has already made him laugh. There is time to find out what brought them over.
