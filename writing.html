Why he writes

The stories let him say what he means, then leave the reader room to recognise an affinity.

A writer within the fiction

The books are part of how Sire becomes known.

Sire is a writer inside the story. His books let women discover the life he hopes to explore: particular desires, continuing travel and the possibilities of larger relationships.

A reader gets to know his way of thinking through the humour, the desires and the ideas that stay with her. Perhaps she wonders what it would be like to meet the person behind the pages. The writing gives that curiosity something substantial to work with.

Writing as a signal

Luke’s intention for the books is personal as well as creative: to find women who naturally harmonise with what he is expressing, through shared interest in science-fiction romantasy. The work is how he makes himself discoverable.

That same idea belongs to Sire. He carries on writing and living. A reader wants a conversation and starts one.

A travelling life, openly expressed

The books make his intentions clear: Sire is not looking to settle down. He wants a life of travel, creativity, desire and relationships across countries. He hopes a reader recognises something of her own desires in that life before she approaches him.

Stories that continue

A first invitation changes the day. A reunion changes what he thought he knew about someone. Across the books, familiar relationships develop alongside new encounters. In a larger marriage, the same arrival means something different to each person waiting for him.

A world worth spending time in

The stories need more than a list of preferences. They need places worth visiting, people with things to say, questions that matter and moments that change a relationship. A hidden city has its own history. A performer has a life after the show. A shared future involves everyday choices as well as extraordinary possibilities.

The Australian Sire Story Forge gathers settings, relationships, rituals, mysteries and connected story possibilities. It is a place to develop the fiction and follow the echoes of one encounter through another story.

Explore the Australian Sire Story Forge ↗

The universe is in development. This site introduces its character and possibilities; the Forge opens the workshop behind them.

That universe reaches through humanity’s growth across planets, Kardashev ambitions and encounters with extraterrestrial and extra-dimensional civilisations. Sire writes about the lives inside that transformation: who meets, what draws them together and how a relationship continues when the next journey leaves Earth behind.
