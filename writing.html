Why he writes

The stories let him say what he means, then leave the reader room to recognise an affinity.

A writer within the fiction

The books are part of how Sire becomes known.

Sire is a writer inside the story. He puts his interests, intentions and imagination into books that women can discover for themselves.

A reader has time to get to know his way of thinking. She can enjoy the humour, recognise a desire, disagree with an idea or wonder what it would be like to meet the person behind the pages. The writing gives that curiosity something substantial to work with.

Writing as a signal

Luke’s intention for the books is personal as well as creative: to find women who naturally harmonise with what he is expressing, through shared interest in science-fiction romantasy. The work is how he makes himself discoverable.

That same idea belongs to Sire. He carries on writing and living. A reader who wants a conversation can be the one to begin it.

A world worth spending time in

The stories need more than a list of preferences. They need places worth visiting, people with things to say, questions that matter and moments that change a relationship. A hidden city has its own history. A performer has a life after the show. A shared future involves everyday choices as well as extraordinary possibilities.

The Australian Sire Story Forge gathers settings, relationships, rituals, mysteries and connected story possibilities. It is a place to develop the fiction and explore how one encounter can echo through another story.
