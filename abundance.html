Joyful responsible abundance

A good life has room for pleasure, useful work, generosity and the people who make it worth living.

What abundance feels like

Time, capability, creativity and a generous everyday life.

For Sire, abundance means the freedom to travel, create and participate in a larger shared life. Time to finish the conversation. The means to meet again. Skills and resources that make more possible together.

The pleasure of having enough

Sire’s ambitions include travel, intimacy, beautiful places and the freedom to follow a promising idea. They also include the everyday things that make those ambitions enjoyable: rest, good meals, friendship and time without constant pressure.

For Sire, abundance includes the freedom to keep travelling and the means to participate in relationships across distance. A welcoming table might be part of a visit, a reunion or a stretch of travelling together. Shared places can hold books, music, affection, learning and practical work while his journey continues.

A shared life across borders

Global Group Marriages brings desire, marriage, pregnancy, children and shared resources into this exploration. It asks what becomes possible when people can make time for one another across several places, enjoy being together and contribute to the life they are creating.

Capability makes room

The wider Aura work asks what becomes possible when people have better access to intelligence, tools, learning and local resources. In Sire’s universe, those ideas can change daily life and the kinds of futures people can choose together.

He wants to meet, share, learn and teach. Contributing is part of his pleasure in being somewhere. It might mean helping a project, sharing a skill, making a useful introduction or bringing a story to a gathering.

Responsibility in ordinary choices

Time, attention and care matter. So do promises, the people affected by a decision and the work needed to keep something going. His idea of abundance includes the capacity to look after what he values.

More life to enjoy. More room to share it.
