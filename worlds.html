The worlds around him

Hidden cities. Ocean civilisations. Intelligent companions. Places with lives already underway.

From the Story Forge

Settings and ideas available to the fiction, with new scenes imagined for this site.

The Forge gives Sire a world much larger than the next destination. Its settings can change what a meeting means and what a shared future could become.

Lives beyond the doorway

A cryptoterrestrial envoy can introduce a society hidden within the familiar world. A sub-oceanic technate makes room for engineering, everyday work and relationships beneath the sea. A worldbuilding festival brings people together through the futures they want to imagine.

Those places need inhabitants, routines, disagreements, food, music and ways of welcoming a stranger. Sire’s arrival is one event in a world that already has plenty happening.

Aura and experience

Aura brings questions of attention, memory, learning and intelligence into the universe. Companions, wearables, geodes and distributed minds give the fiction different ways to explore what it means to share experience.

The seven-torus spaces and hollow geosphere offer a distinctive visual language. Their geometry can shape rooms, instruments and ways of navigating information, giving the imagined technology a physical presence.

Music and shared futures

Music can carry a character’s feeling, introduce a scene or make an encounter memorable. The i C. infinity music universe is one of the creative threads connected to this larger world.

Global Group Marriages opens another line of exploration: shared homes, international relationships, children, care and future generations. These ideas give the stories practical and emotional questions to live with.
