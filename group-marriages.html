Global Group Marriages

Love, desire and shared commitments across countries. Sire’s life keeps moving.

An expansive relationship exploration

A travelling writer, ongoing relationships and a world of places to meet again.

Global Group Marriages opens an expansive possibility in Sire’s life: several adults sharing love, sexuality, family and resources across countries and cultures. His travels continue within that imagined relationship.

Several people, several places

The wider proposal begins with three or more adults understanding their relationships as a larger marriage. Romantic, sexual, companionate, creative and parenting bonds each have their own place. In Sire’s fiction, those relationships stretch between connected homes, shared journeys and reunions.

He arrives with stories from the journey. Someone has news she wanted to tell him in person. Another spouse is leaving tomorrow. Their lives overlap without following one itinerary.

Desire and future generations

Attraction, fertility, pregnancy and making and raising children together belong within this exploration. Sire’s hopes for fatherhood meet the desires and decisions of the people involved. Affection, humour, shared work and the pleasure of being wanted give those relationships their everyday life.

The United Nations of Love

The United Nations of Love is a marriage simulacrum of the United Nations. Its members are self-sovereign adult spouses from many cultures, sharing an intimate life and raising children together. A marriage covenant and family assembly bring the international metaphor into the household.

Languages, stories, food and celebrations meet around the same table. Diplomacy is practised between lovers and spouses; collective intelligence includes sexuality, parenting, practical experience and the knowledge each person brings. Future generations are their own children, with faces and lives in the room.

From the intimate to the planetary

GAJRA Earth carries the exploration of distinct lives in relationship towards joyful responsible abundance at a planetary scale. The marriage simulacrum is one possible expression within that much larger field of potential.

The journey stays open

A reunion, a travelling companion or an invitation into an existing marriage each opens a different story. Sire remains a writer and traveller. These relationships develop through the people involved, while other encounters and ways of living remain in view.

The wider framework and relationship models develop the proposal. The World Law Atlas examines present-day recognition and practical questions. The Story Forge explores the imagined lives.
