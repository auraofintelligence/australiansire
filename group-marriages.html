Global Group Marriages

Love, desire and shared commitments across countries. Sire’s life keeps moving.

An expansive relationship exploration

A travelling writer, ongoing relationships and a world of places to meet again.

Sire is not the settling-down kind. Global Group Marriages opens one substantial part of his imagined horizon: love, sexual desire, marriage and future generations across countries and cultures, within a journey that keeps opening.

He wants to experience every country and territory. Along the way, women initiate encounters that may become a shared adventure, an ongoing relationship or an invitation into a larger marriage. The possibilities grow through those meetings.

Several people, several places

The wider Global Group Marriages proposal begins with three or more adults choosing to understand their relationships as a larger marriage. It explores romantic, sexual, companionate and creative bonds, alongside relationships centred on parenting. Each connection contributes something different to their shared life.

In Sire’s imagined life, the marriage spans countries and connected homes. Some people travel together; others meet again in a place they know or somewhere new. Each relationship has its own history, attraction and rhythm, while the wider group develops things they share.

For Sire, a visit deepens a relationship while his travels continue. Returning brings its own excitement. There is someone he wants to see, a conversation to continue, a celebration to join or something they have been looking forward to doing together.

More people, more possibilities

Global Group Marriages explores affection, intimacy, practical support, shared income, places to stay and a wider mix of skills. Someone’s pleasure in travelling sits alongside another person’s love of teaching, making music, growing food or creating a welcoming place.

Sire brings writing, curiosity, experience and the connections he makes along the way. The people he meets bring their own capabilities and ambitions. The series explores how that shared capacity gives them more time, freedom and opportunities to enjoy life together.

Desire and future generations

The exploration includes sexual attraction, fertility, pregnancy and the possibility of making and raising children together. These are substantial themes in his imagined future. They sit alongside affection, humour, cultural exchange and the pleasure of being wanted.

That gives the fiction personal questions to explore: how do they share time across distance, what do they want from one another, and what does being involved look like as their lives change? Where children become part of a story, their care and relationships continue through the journeys too.

The world keeps opening

The travel oracle brings a gathering, a reader’s invitation, a creative opportunity and time with an existing partner into the same journey. The Forge connects Global Group Marriages with local groups and meetups, making relationships part of how a worthwhile next destination comes into view.

The United Nations of Love

The United Nations of Love is a marriage simulacrum of the United Nations. Its members are self-sovereign adult spouses from many cultures, sharing a loving, sexually alive marriage and making and raising children together. A marriage covenant, a family assembly and the everyday exchange of care, resources and knowledge bring the international metaphor into intimate life.

Languages, food, stories, celebrations and ways of thinking meet around the same table. A shared decision travels into the rest of their day, between spouses with dinner, desire and tomorrow to share. Collective intelligence includes affection, sexuality, pregnancy, parenting, practical work and the experience each person brings.

Within this possible story, Sire’s travels connect the marriage with the wider world. His desire, writing and hopes for fatherhood belong within it. Peace and future generations acquire faces, relationships and consequences he lives with. Other encounters and ways of living remain part of the universe’s open scope.

GAJRA Earth

GAJRA Earth carries that shared thread to the planetary scale: distinct people, cultures, places and intelligences participating in joyful responsible abundance. The larger marriage, the United Nations of Love simulacrum and the planetary vision offer connected scales of intimate and collective life within the fiction’s wider scope of potential.

As the fictional universe opens beyond Earth, those relationships travel with it. A family spans planets. Artificial intelligences and robots participate in daily work. Encounters with extraterrestrial and extra-dimensional civilisations bring unfamiliar ways of loving, thinking and living into the conversation.

This gives Sire’s books a way to connect the personal with the planetary. The Forge takes another leap with a family spread across Earth, orbit and off-world places, where relationships continue across light-minutes. The imagined future reaches far beyond Earth while the story stays close to the people living it.

Stories that continue between visits

A group of readers might invite him to a gathering after recognising something of their own hopes in his books. Partners might travel with him for part of a journey. A return visit might bring a shared celebration or a conversation that changes their plans. These are possible story directions, with the people and their choices shaping what follows.

Sire keeps writing as that life unfolds. Global Group Marriages gives the series an ongoing thread: where does a shared life lead while he continues exploring?

Explore the idea further

The Global Group Marriages framework develops shared love, care, resources and choice. Its relationship models include connected homes and marriages across countries. The U.N. of Love and GAJRA Earth extend those ideas into cross-cultural and planetary life.

The World Law Atlas examines present-day recognition, parentage, property, movement and care country by country. Sire’s fiction explores imagined lives through the Story Forge.
